The Plunge

Water is roughly 800 times denser than air. That resistance is free strength training — no dumbbells required.

Why Swim?

A full-body workout, minus the pounding

Swimming trains the heart and lungs, builds strength through natural resistance, and improves flexibility with every reach and kick. Because water supports body weight, it's one of the rare exercises that's gentle on joints while still demanding of muscle — which is why it works for a 9-year-old and a 90-year-old alike.

What you gain in the water

  • ✓ Stronger heart & lungs
  • ✓ Full-body muscle tone
  • ✓ High calorie burn
  • ✓ Lower stress, better sleep
  • ✓ Joint-friendly at any age
